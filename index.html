01 / RESEARCH

Research interests

01

Generalized symmetries
& quantum phases

Fusion category symmetries, their on-site realizations, and the classification and construction of non-invertible symmetry-protected topological phases.

02

Tensor network
renormalization

Symmetry-aware coarse-graining algorithms for statistical systems and lattice field theories, with practical implementations in Julia.

03

Conformal field theory
& holographic duality

Algebraic approaches to critical fixed points and the extraction of conformal data, organized by anyon sector, from tensor networks.
